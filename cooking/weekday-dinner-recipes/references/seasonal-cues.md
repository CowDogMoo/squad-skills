# Seasonal cues

Use these to bias candidate selection. The point isn't to be rigid — it's to make the batch *feel* like the season.

## Spring (Mar–May)

- Produce: asparagus, peas, artichokes, spring onions, radishes, strawberries, lemon, fresh herbs (mint, dill, chives), arugula
- Proteins: lamb, salmon, eggs, chicken
- Styles: light pastas, frittatas, sheet-pan, simple roasts, vinaigrette-dressed grain bowls
- Avoid: heavy braises, rich stews, soup-as-meal

## Summer (Jun–Aug)

- Produce: corn, tomatoes (esp. cherry/heirloom), zucchini, peaches, basil, cucumber, peppers, eggplant, watermelon
- Proteins: shrimp, salmon, chicken thighs, flank/skirt steak, halloumi, tofu
- Styles: grill, no-oven (skillet, cold noodle, salad-as-meal), tacos, sheet pan
- Avoid: anything that runs the oven over 400°F for an hour, slow braises, casseroles

## Fall (Sep–Nov)

- Produce: squash (butternut, delicata, acorn), apples, pears, brussels sprouts, mushrooms, sweet potato, kale, cauliflower
- Proteins: roast chicken, pork, beef, white fish, lentils, beans
- Styles: sheet-pan roasts, hearty grain bowls, skillet braises, stuffed squash, soups (still light enough for weeknight)
- Avoid: very light cold dishes, peak-summer ingredients

## Winter (Dec–Feb)

- Produce: citrus (lemons, oranges, grapefruit), cabbage, leeks, parsnips, persimmons, hardy greens, root veg
- Proteins: braising cuts (within weeknight time), chicken thighs, white beans, lentils, salmon
- Styles: one-pot, sheet-pan, stews-that-fit-in-45-min, baked pasta, brothy bowls (pho/ramen-style), curries
- Avoid: outdoor grilling (unless user has it), summer-specific produce

## Cross-season principles

- "Weeknight" means ~45 min start-to-table, excluding hands-off marinade time
- Variety in the batch matters more than perfect seasonal purity — include 1–2 "shoulder season" dishes if they improve the mix
- A grill recipe in shoulder months (early spring, late fall) is fine if the user has indoor grilling
